## Google Play

### App name

**PiggyAI**

### Short description

Private expense tracking with on-device bank SMS detection and no cloud sync.

### Full description

PiggyAI is a private, offline-first expense manager built for everyday money tracking in India.

On Android, PiggyAI can detect bank and payment transaction alerts from SMS after you explicitly enable SMS access. SMS parsing happens entirely on your device. Matching alerts are added to a Pending Review inbox so you can confirm, edit, categorize, or remove them before they affect budgets or reports. Unmatched messages are not stored by PiggyAI.

You can also use PiggyAI without SMS access. Manual debit and credit entry works on Android and iOS, and iOS uses manual entry because third-party apps do not have access to the SMS inbox.

Key features:

- Android bank-SMS transaction detection with explicit user consent
- Pending Review before detected transactions affect your records
- clear Debit/Credit direction plus Expense, Income, Transfer, Borrowed, Loan, EMI, Refund, Investment, Cash and other transaction purposes
- merchant-to-category learning from your confirmed corrections
- duplicate transaction warnings without silent deletion
- custom categories and INR budgets
- recurring income/expense planning and private local bill reminders
- subscription suggestions based only on your confirmed local history
- safe-to-spend guidance and deterministic local forecasts
- savings goals, weekly money quests, monthly insights and cash-flow calendar
- encrypted local backup export and restore preview
- privacy masking and optional biometric/device lock
- full local data deletion

Privacy is a product feature, not an add-on. PiggyAI has no PiggyAI account, backend, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, telemetry, or remote AI. Sensitive text is encrypted locally and the Android production app does not request the INTERNET permission.

Your SMS and financial data are not sent to PiggyAI servers because PiggyAI has no financial-data server. If you export an encrypted backup, you choose the destination yourself using the system share flow.

PiggyAI is guidance and record-keeping software. It does not connect to a bank account, move money, provide credit, execute investments, or guarantee financial outcomes.

## Review notes

### Google Play review note

PiggyAI's Android SMS permissions are used only for its core SMS-based money-management feature. The app displays a dedicated disclosure immediately before requesting SMS permission. READ_SMS supports a user-triggered scan of recent financial alerts and RECEIVE_SMS supports detection of new bank/payment alerts after the user enables the feature. Parsing is local; matching alerts become pending review transactions; unmatched messages are not stored; data is not transmitted off-device. Manual entry remains available without SMS permission.

### App Store review note

PiggyAI is offline-first and the iOS build does not access SMS. Transactions are entered manually. There is no app account, backend, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, telemetry or remote AI. Encrypted backup export is user initiated, and the Privacy Policy is available inside Settings.
